# VIBE 0.5.5 — Account deletion

Date: 2026-10-26

Status: Included in 0.6.0; not released separately.

Settings now include a danger area for permanent account deletion under Account & App. Before proceeding, members see a localized explanation of the data removed, a warning that deletion cannot be undone, and a note that previously known direct media links may remain accessible. The member must type DELETE (LÖSCHEN in German) and can cancel without changing the account.

Deletion is bound to the current authenticated profile, uses the existing transactional cleanup for its VIBE data, removes linked credentials and provider identities, clears device-local drafts where browser storage allows, and signs the member out. Protected administrator and system profiles cannot be self-deleted. Messages from other people remain in their accounts.
