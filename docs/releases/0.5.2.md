# VIBE 0.5.2 — VIBE Reflex und VIBE Stack

Date: 2026-10-26
Status: Web release. Android and Windows remain at local 0.5.1 debug candidates.

The Games hub has a VIBE-styled library with previews and dedicated game pages. VIBE Reflex is a five-round reaction test: wait for the randomly timed green signal, tap or press Space, and replay a round after a false start. Completed runs show average and fastest response time; results are not ranked.

VIBE Stack is playable on a 10×20 board with VIBE-logo pieces, keyboard and touch controls, hold and next previews, line clears, levels, scoring, and a device-local best score. Pieces move through clear cells and fit below overhangs when the target cells are open. The game uses standard gravity, rotation, soft drop and hard drop behavior. Browser storage is optional: gameplay continues if the local best score cannot be read or saved, and an empty piece queue refills safely.

The Reflex help orb uses the same 150 ms entrance and reverse exit animation as Quick Settings. Its three-second idle-close fallback pauses while hovered or focused, restarts after interaction ends, and remains cancellable by reopening the orb during the exit.

VIBE Trail, VIBE Glide, and leaderboards remain future plans in the TODO.
